# AgriConnect (एग्रीकनेक्ट) 🌾🇮🇳
### Smart Climate Crop Advisory & Certified Agri-Input Marketplace for Indian Farmers

AgriConnect is a comprehensive web application designed for farmers across India, connecting seasonal agro-climatic advisories with a direct supply chain of certified seeds, nutritional fertilizers, small agricultural implements, and bio-care products.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🌦️ Climate & Season-Based Crop Advisory System
- **Detailed Coverage of All 4 Indian Agro-Climatic Zones**:
  - **North India (उत्तर भारत)**: Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir.
  - **South India (दक्षिण भारत)**: Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Kerala.
  - **West India (पश्चिम भारत)**: Maharashtra, Gujarat, Goa, Madhya Pradesh.
  - **East & Northeast India (पूर्वी भारत)**: West Bengal, Bihar, Odisha, Jharkhand, Assam.
- **Cropping Seasons**: Kharif (Monsoon), Rabi (Winter), Zaid (Summer), Aus/Aman/Boro, and Plantation cycles.
- **Scientific Recommendations per Crop**:
  - Top hybrid high-yield varieties (e.g., Wheat HD-3086/DBW-187, Basmati Rice Pusa 1121, Samba Mahsuri BPT 5204, Soybean KDS-726, Mustard Pusa Bold, Bhima Super Onion).
  - Sowing & Harvesting calendar windows.
  - Soil compatibility (Alluvial, Black Cotton / Regur, Red Loam, Sandy Loam, Clayey, Laterite).
  - NPK nutrient ratios, irrigation schedules, and disease management tips.
  - Direct 1-click links to buy compatible seeds and inputs from the AgriConnect store.

### 2. 🛒 E-Commerce & Agricultural Input Store
- **Certified Seeds**: High-germination (>85%), NSC & ICAR certified varieties.
- **Fertilizers & Nutrients**: 100% Water-Soluble NPK 19:19:19, IFFCO Nano Urea Liquid, Enriched Vermicompost, Zinc Sulphate 33%, Bio-NPK Consortium.
- **Small Agricultural Equipment**:
  - 16-Liter 12V Battery-Operated Knapsack Sprayer.
  - Manual Push-Type Precision Seed & Fertilizer Planter Drill.
  - 4-Stroke 50cc Portable Crop Harvester & Brush Cutter.
  - 4-in-1 Digital Soil Moisture, pH & Lux Meter.
  - Gravity Drip Irrigation Starter Kit (0.5 Acre).
  - Solar LED Insect Light Traps.
- **Government Subsidy Tags**: Highlighting equipment eligible for 40-50% subsidy under SMAM (Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization).
- **Interactive Cart & Checkout**:
  - Doorstep village-level delivery tracking.
  - Cash on Delivery (COD), Kisan Credit Card (KCC), and UPI payment options.
  - Instant generated Order ID and SMS/WhatsApp confirmation simulation.

### 3. ⚖️ Interactive Soil & Fertilizer (NPK) Calculator
- Enter land size in Acres or Hectares.
- Select crop and soil fertility level.
- Computes exact commercial bags of Urea (45kg), DAP (50kg), MOP Potash (50kg), Zinc Sulphate, or Nano Urea bottles.
- Provides a step-by-step split application schedule (Basal dose at sowing, CRI/Tillering top-dressing, and Pre-flowering spray).

### 4. 🛰️ 7-Day Regional Weather & Pest Alerts
- Real-time simulated temperature, humidity, rainfall forecast, and phenological crop stage warnings (e.g., Aphid alerts in mustard, Late Blight in potato, BPH in paddy).

### 5. 🏛️ Government Schemes & 24x7 Helplines
- Direct integration details for PM-KISAN, SMAM Subsidy, PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY), and Soil Health Card scheme.
- One-click call button for **Toll-Free Kisan Call Centre (1800-180-1551)**.
- WhatsApp Krishi Mitra expert support connect.
